2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect or process the following:

  • Account information: Information associated with your account, such as your name, email address, Firebase user identifier (UID) and account creation date.
  • Quiz information: Quiz subject and chapter, correct and incorrect answer counts, submitted responses, time taken and result date.
  • Authentication information: Information processed by Firebase Authentication when you register or sign in using email and password or Google sign-in. We do not receive your Google account password.
  • Advertising and technical information: If advertisements are enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and its service providers may process information such as IP address, advertising identifiers, interactions with advertisements, diagnostics and device-related information, as described in their policies.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect account access.
  • Save and display users' quiz results and progress.
  • Operate, maintain and improve the App.
  • Display advertisements when advertising is enabled.
  • Identify technical problems and help protect the service.

4. Firebase and Data Storage

Cyber Quiz uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Account and quiz information is stored and processed using these services.

Quiz results are associated with the relevant Firebase user identifier. Access is controlled through Firebase security rules. We do not intend ordinary users to access another user's private quiz history.

5. Advertising

Cyber Quiz may display advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Advertising may be disabled in some app builds or environments.

When advertisements are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process information required to provide, measure, protect and improve advertising services. The information processed may include device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic information. The information and choices available to users may depend on their location, device settings and applicable consent requirements.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

6. Account Deletion and Data Removal

You can request deletion of your Cyber Quiz account through the account deletion option available inside the App.

The account deletion process is designed to delete the user's associated quiz result documents, user profile document and Firebase Authentication account.

If you cannot access the App, you may contact us at singla.arvind12@gmail.com and request account and associated data deletion. Please use the email address associated with your account so that we can verify your request.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups, security logs or other records where retention is required or permitted by law or by the relevant service provider.
